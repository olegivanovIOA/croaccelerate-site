For the last ten months we mostly didn't talk about our work. We did it.

Inside a fast-growing manufacturing group we started from almost nothing: management data lived in spreadsheets, reports were assembled by hand, and every meeting began with the question "which number is right?". Today the group runs on two live dashboards, one registry of 168 metrics that refresh every hour, AI agents that do real work, and a finance contour that builds cash flow, P&L and a balance sheet from the same data layer.

Our old website described none of that. So we rebuilt it.

## What changed

**The positioning.** We started as a fractional commercial (CCO) practice. That is still one of our services, but it is no longer the centre. Today the honest description is **Fractional Chief Innovation Officer**: one accountable owner for AI, data and digital operations, working part-time with a mature team of 12+ experts — engineers, data and AI specialists, analysts and growth practitioners — who build what the roadmap says.

**The services.** Seven of them, each with a clear first deliverable and a timeline:

- **AI Agents** — a first agent on your real data in 3 working days.
- **Chatbots** — for a trade show, HR, PR or sales, live in 2–3 days.
- **AI Office** — a team of agents coordinated by an Office Secretary.
- **AI Project Assistants** — Gantt, tasks, resources, risk checks, project cash flow and P&L, a weekly digest for every project.
- **Digital Control Tower** — the first version of a live management dashboard in week one.
- **Revenue Engine / Fractional CCO** — closed-loop attribution and a sales system that turns spend into traceable revenue.
- And the role that ties it together — **Fractional Chief Innovation Officer**.

**The design.** Calmer and lighter. Less decoration, more substance: real structure, diagrams and mock dashboards that mirror what we built.

## Five cases from ten months

Every case is anonymised. No company names, no products, no money. What remains is what matters to a reader: the problem, the approach, the integrations, the scope — and what didn't go to plan.

1. **[Two live dashboards for a manufacturing group](/cases/manufacturing-group-control-tower/)** — 2 companies, 8 departments, 168 metrics in one registry, 81% automated, refreshed hourly.
2. **[An AI Office with a Secretary](/cases/ai-office-of-agents/)** — seven specialist agents, each on the AI model that fits its job, including three agents for key accounts.
3. **[Closed-loop marketing attribution](/cases/closed-loop-marketing-attribution/)** — four ad accounts joined to CRM revenue, ROMI by revenue and by margin.
4. **[A finance agent for CF, P&L and balance sheet](/cases/multi-entity-finance-automation/)** — several legal entities, one transaction layer, three statements and a management toolkit on top.
5. **[Market intelligence for EU expansion](/cases/market-intelligence-eu-expansion/)** — roughly 160,000 import records turned into a short list of countries and partners.

## Why we show what didn't work

Most case studies are a catalogue of wins. Ours are not, on purpose. The cases have a section called "What didn't go to plan": early dashboard blocks marked "live" that still held placeholder figures; a contractor's report that stopped updating and nobody noticed for days; a date filter that quietly dropped a few lines from one entity's month until it was rebuilt; a clever lead-split model we threw away because the team couldn't act on it.

That is where the real learning is — and it is what a CEO wants to know before trusting anyone with their data.

## How we look at a company

One idea runs through every case: value rarely leaks inside a department. It leaks at the seams — where one function's responsibility ends and another starts paying for its result. Procurement saves, production pays in defects. Marketing delivers leads, sales can't close them. Every report is right, and the company still misses its number.

We wrote about it in more detail in [Every Department Hit Its KPI. The Company Missed Its Number](/blog/every-department-hit-its-kpi/).
