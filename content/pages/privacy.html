This policy explains what personal data CRO Accelerate collects through croaccelerate.com and how it is used. The data controller is Oleg Ivanov, a registered sole proprietor (FOP) in Ukraine. Contact for all privacy questions: {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • Contact and scorecard forms: name, work email, company, role, company size, country, your message and — for the AI Readiness Scorecard — your answers and score. Also technical context: page, referrer and campaign parameters (UTM, click IDs).
  • Call bookings through Calendly: the details you enter there (Calendly's own privacy policy applies).
  • Analytics and advertising cookies (Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, LinkedIn Insight Tag) — only after you consent in the cookie banner if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland.

Why and on what legal basis

  • To answer your request and prepare a proposal or your personal roadmap — steps prior to a contract / legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b), (f) GDPR).
  • To send occasional insights if you ticked the box — consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); unsubscribe at any time.
  • To measure which content and campaigns are useful — consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) via Google Consent Mode.

Processors

Google (Workspace, Analytics, Tag Manager, Ads), GoDaddy (hosting), Calendly (scheduling), LinkedIn (ads measurement, if consented) and Anthropic (AI assistance used to draft replies and roadmaps, reviewed by a person before sending; data is not used to train models). Some processors are located outside the EEA; transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

Retention

Lead data is kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, then deleted or anonymised. Analytics data follows the retention set in Google Analytics (14 months).

International transfers

The controller is established in Ukraine. Personal data of EU/EEA and UK visitors is processed only to answer requests and is transferred to the processors listed above under their standard contractual safeguards.
